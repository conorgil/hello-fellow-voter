Hello, Fellow Voter!

Thank you for helping to get out the vote by sending hand written postcards, letters, voting guides, and more to prospective voters!

This app will makes the writing process easier by showing each voter address in large font and tracking the status of each voter in your list.

  1. Import your voter address list by uploading a PDF
  2. Click a voter and their addreess will be shown in large font so it is easy to copy
  3. Click Next Voter to move straight to the next address
  4. Repeat!

Tips and tricks

  • Select multiple individual voters at once by clicking each checkbox or by holding Ctrl/Cmd while clicking
  • Select a range of voters in a column by holding Shift and clicking.

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
→ (right arrow) While a voter's address it open, move to the next voter
Esc Close the address view or clear the current voter selection
Ctrl+Z / Cmd+Z Undo
Ctrl+Shift+Z / Cmd+Shift+Z / Ctrl+Y Redo

You can reopen this page any time from the ? button in the top-right corner.
