Hello, Fellow Voter!

We love that you are helping to get out the vote by sending hand written postcards, letters, voting guides, and more to prospective voters.

This app should help make that process easier by showing you the voter addresses one-by-one in large font and keeping track of the status of each voter in your list.

Writing postcards

  1. Import your voter address list by uploading a PDF
  2. Click a voter and their addreess will be shown in large font so it is easy to copy
  3. Click Next Voter to move straight to the next address
  4. Repeat!

Selecting and moving multiple cards

  • Click a card's checkbox, or Ctrl/Cmd-click a card, to select it.
  • Shift-click to select a range of cards in a column.
  • Use a column's Select all link to select every card in that column.
  • Drag any selected card to move the whole selection to another column at once.
