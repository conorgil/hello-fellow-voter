Hello, Fellow Voter!

This app smooths the process of hand writing postcards and letters to prospective voters.

Everything runs locally in your browser and no data gets sent remotely.

  1. Import the list of voter names/addresses that you received from the organization you are volunteering with (e.g., Signs of Justice, Vote Forward, etc).
  2. Click a voter and their addreess will be shown in large font so it is easy to copy.
  3. Click Next Voter or press the right arrow key to move to the next address.
  4. Repeat!

Tips and tricks

  • Select multiple individual voters at once by clicking each checkbox or by holding Ctrl/Cmd while clicking.
  • Select a range of voters in a column by holding Shift and clicking.

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
→ (right arrow) While a voter's address is open, move to the next voter
Esc Close the address view or clear the current voter selection
Ctrl+Z / Cmd+Z Undo
Ctrl+Shift+Z / Cmd+Shift+Z / Ctrl+Y Redo

This app is not associated with any other organization. It is meant to help you remain organized while you volunteer with other organizations that focus on contacting voters by mail. Everything runs locally in your browser and no data gets sent remotely, so the organization you are volunteering with will not receive any updates on your progress as you complete and mail your postcards and letters. We recommend reporting your progress back to those organizations directly so that they can know which voters were actually contacted.

You can reopen this page any time from the ? button in the top-right corner.
