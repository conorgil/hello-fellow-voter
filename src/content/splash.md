# Welcome to Postcard Buddy

Postcard Buddy helps you track hand-written "get out the vote" postcards from a voter list PDF all the way to mailed.

Upload a PDF for a project and it's parsed into a board of cards, one per voter, moving through five stages:

**TODO → Writing → Written → Stamp Applied → Mailed**

## Writing postcards

1. Click a card in the **TODO** column to open it. It automatically moves to **Writing**, and the name/address are shown large so they're easy to copy onto the postcard.
2. When you've finished writing it, click **Next voter** (or press the right-arrow key). The card moves to **Written**, and the next card in your TODO queue is pulled into Writing and opened automatically — so you can keep going without leaving the detail view.
3. Once a batch of postcards is stamped and mailed, drag the cards (or select several at once) into the **Stamp Applied** and **Mailed** columns.

## Selecting and moving multiple cards

- Click a card's checkbox, or **Ctrl/Cmd-click** a card, to select it.
- **Shift-click** to select a range of cards in a column.
- Use a column's **Select all** link to select every card in that column.
- Drag any selected card to move the whole selection to another column at once.

## Keyboard shortcuts

| Shortcut | Action |
| --- | --- |
| `→` (right arrow) | While a card's detail view is open, same as clicking "Next voter" |
| `Esc` | Close the detail view, or clear the current card selection |
| `Ctrl+Z` / `Cmd+Z` | Undo the last change |
| `Ctrl+Shift+Z` / `Cmd+Shift+Z` / `Ctrl+Y` | Redo |

You can reopen this page any time from the **?** button in the top-right corner.
